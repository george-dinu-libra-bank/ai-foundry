# Lost or stolen card

Speed matters here more than anywhere else in card servicing, because the customer's
liability depends on when the report was made.

## Reporting

A lost or stolen card can be reported through three channels, all available 24 hours a
day, every day:

- the Libra Assist Mobile app, **Cards → Report lost or stolen** — the fastest route, and
  the block is applied immediately;
- the Card Support line on **0800 800 210**, free from any Romanian network;
- any Libra Bank branch during opening hours.

A report made by any channel blocks the card permanently. Unlike a temporary freeze, this
block cannot be lifted — a card reported lost or stolen is dead even if it is found the
next day, and a replacement must be ordered.

## Liability

Transactions made **before** the report are the customer's liability up to a maximum of
**300 lei**, unless the customer acted fraudulently or with gross negligence, in which case
the full amount is their liability. Writing the PIN on the card or keeping it in the same
wallet counts as gross negligence.

Transactions made **after** the report are entirely the bank's liability. This is why the
timestamp of the report is recorded to the second and given to the customer.

## What happens next

1. The card is blocked permanently and the block is confirmed by SMS.
2. A replacement card is ordered under the *Card replacement procedure*. The fee for a
   lost or stolen replacement is in the current fee schedule.
3. A **new PIN** is generated and sent by SMS separately from the card.
4. Any transaction the customer disputes is handled under *Disputed transactions*, with
   the 13-month deadline running from the transaction date.
5. If the card was stolen together with identity documents, the customer is advised to
   file a police report; the bank does not file it on their behalf.

## Abroad

A customer stranded abroad without a card can request an **emergency replacement**, which
is couriered to a foreign address within 3 working days and carries the emergency
replacement fee in the current fee schedule. In most cases a **virtual card**, issued in
the app in minutes and usable in Apple Pay or Google Pay, solves the problem faster and
free of charge.
