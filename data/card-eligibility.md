# Card eligibility criteria

Who may hold which Libra Bank card. This document says nothing about what each card
costs — for that, read the current *Card fee schedule*. Eligibility and price are
deliberately kept apart, because eligibility changes rarely and prices change yearly.

## Common requirements

Every card applicant must:

- be at least **14 years old** for a debit card, or **18 years old** for a credit card;
- hold an open Libra Bank current account in lei;
- be a resident of Romania, or a non-resident with a Romanian tax identification number;
- have a valid identity document on file, not expiring in the next 30 days.

## Libra Standard Debit

The default card. Available to anyone meeting the common requirements. No income
condition. Applicants aged 14 to 17 receive a Standard Debit card with the online
payment channel disabled by default; a parent or guardian enables it in branch.

## Libra Plus Debit

Requires the common requirements plus **either** a net monthly income of at least
**4,000 lei** credited to a Libra Bank account for the last three consecutive months,
**or** an average current account balance above **10,000 lei** over the last six months.
A customer who stops meeting the condition is not downgraded automatically; the condition
is checked only at application and at the three-yearly card renewal.

## Libra Gold Credit

Requires the common requirements plus:

- a minimum age of **18 years**;
- a net monthly income of at least **3,500 lei**, documented by a payslip or a tax
  statement not older than 60 days;
- a clean record in the Credit Bureau, meaning no payment more than 30 days late in the
  last 12 months;
- a total debt service below **40%** of net monthly income after the new card's assumed
  limit is included.

The assigned credit limit is between **1,500 lei** and **six times** the documented net
monthly income, capped at 50,000 lei.

## Libra Virtual

Available to any customer who already holds an active Libra Bank debit or credit card. It
cannot be the first and only card on an account, because it is funded from the parent
card's account and inherits its limits.

## Refusals

An application may be refused without a reason being given, and refusal is not appealable
through Card Support. A customer who wants the decision reviewed submits a written request
in branch; the review takes up to 15 working days.
