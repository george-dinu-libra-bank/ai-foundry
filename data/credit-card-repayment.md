# Gold Credit repayment and interest

This document covers only the Libra Gold Credit card. Debit cards have no statement, no
minimum payment and no interest.

## The statement cycle

The statement closes on the **last calendar day of each month**. The statement is issued on
the first working day of the following month and is available in the app and by email. The
payment due date is the **15th day** of the month following the statement close. If the
15th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the due date moves to the next working day.

## Minimum payment

The minimum payment is **5% of the statement balance, or 50 lei, whichever is greater** —
plus the full amount of any fees, interest and past-due amounts from earlier cycles. A
statement balance below 50 lei must be paid in full.

Paying only the minimum keeps the account in good standing but loses the interest-free
period on the entire balance, not just the unpaid part.

## Interest-free period

Purchases carry no interest if the **full statement balance** is paid by the due date. The
interest-free period runs from the purchase date to the due date and is therefore up to
**55 days** for a purchase made on the first day of a cycle and as little as 15 days for one
made on the last day.

Cash withdrawals never have an interest-free period. Interest on a cash withdrawal runs
from the day of the withdrawal, even if the balance is paid in full on time.

## Interest rate

The annual interest rate on the Gold Credit card is **22.9%** for purchases and **28.9%**
for cash withdrawals. Interest is calculated daily on the outstanding balance and charged
monthly at statement close.

## Missing a payment

A missed minimum payment triggers the late payment fee in the current fee schedule, charged
once per statement cycle. It is reported to the Credit Bureau once the payment is
**30 days** overdue; a payment 5 days late is not reported.

Three consecutive missed minimum payments allow the bank to suspend the credit line, block
the card and demand the full outstanding balance.

## Paying the card

- Automatic direct debit from a Libra Bank current account, for either the minimum payment
  or the full balance — set up in the app, takes effect from the next cycle.
- Transfer from any bank to the card's IBAN, which is shown on the statement. Allow
  **2 working days** for an inter-bank transfer to land; a payment that arrives on the 16th
  is late even if it was sent on the 14th.
- Cash at a Libra Bank counter, credited the same day.
