# Closing a card

Closing a card is not the same as blocking it. A block stops transactions; a closure ends
the card contract. Closure is irreversible — a closed card cannot be reopened, only
replaced by a new application.

## Before closing

Check all four of these with the customer, in this order:

1. **Recurring payments.** List the merchants billing the card in the last three months and
   tell the customer each one will fail. This is the step most often skipped, and the one
   that generates the complaint.
2. **Pending transactions.** A transaction authorised but not yet settled still settles
   after closure and is debited from the account. Closure does not cancel it.
3. **Outstanding balance**, for a Gold Credit card. The full balance including accrued
   interest must be settled before the card contract ends. A credit card cannot be closed
   with a balance outstanding.
4. **Supplementary cards.** Closing a main card automatically closes every supplementary
   card on it. Holders of supplementary cards are not notified by the bank; the main
   cardholder must tell them.

## Doing it

A debit card is closed in the mobile app under **Cards → Close card**, or in branch. A
credit card can only be closed in branch or in writing, because the credit agreement is
being terminated.

The card stops working immediately. The monthly maintenance fee is charged **pro rata** for
the days of the month the card was open, and appears on the next statement.

## The 45-day window

For 45 days after closure, an incoming refund from a merchant to the closed card is
accepted and credited to the linked account. After 45 days the refund is rejected and
returns to the merchant, and the customer must claim it from the merchant directly. Tell
customers who are closing a card after a dispute to wait until the dispute is resolved.

## Closing the account too

Closing the last card on a current account does not close the account. The account remains
open, keeps its IBAN, keeps receiving transfers, and keeps its own fees. Account closure is
a separate request handled by branch staff, not by Card Support.
