# Card replacement procedure

This procedure applies when a card must be physically reissued: damage, wear, a lost or
stolen card, a name change, or a compromised card number after a fraud incident. It does
not apply to renewal at natural expiry, which happens automatically 30 days before the
expiry date and needs no request.

Follow the steps in order. Skipping step 2 is the single most common cause of a
replacement card being delivered to an address the customer no longer lives at.

1. **Verify the customer's identity.** In branch, check a valid identity document against
   the record. By phone, use the three-question verification: date of birth, the last
   four digits of the card, and the amount of the most recent card transaction. A customer
   who fails verification is never told which answer was wrong.

2. **Confirm the delivery address on file.** Read the address back to the customer in
   full and ask them to confirm it. If it has changed, update the address record first
   and only then continue — an address changed after the card is ordered does not
   redirect the card already in production.

3. **Block the old card.** Select the reason accurately, because the reason drives the
   fee: *damaged*, *lost*, *stolen*, *fraud*, or *administrative* (name change, bank
   error). A card replaced for fraud or bank error carries no fee; see the current fee
   schedule for the others.

4. **Choose the delivery speed.** Standard delivery is by post to the address on file and
   takes **5 to 7 working days**. Express delivery by courier takes **2 working days** and
   carries the express fee in the current fee schedule. Branch collection is available in
   **3 working days** at the branch the customer names.

5. **Order the replacement in the card management system.** The new card keeps the same
   account and the same daily limits, but receives a **new card number, a new expiry date
   and a new CVV**. Tell the customer this explicitly, because it is what breaks their
   recurring payments.

6. **Warn the customer about recurring payments.** Subscriptions, streaming services, and
   any merchant holding the card on file will fail after the number changes. The customer
   must update each merchant once the new card arrives. Tokenised wallets (Apple Pay,
   Google Pay) are re-provisioned automatically within 24 hours of activation and do not
   need updating.

7. **Set the PIN expectation.** The replacement card arrives with the **same PIN** as the
   old one if the replacement reason was damage or wear. For lost, stolen or fraud
   replacements a **new PIN** is generated and sent separately by SMS, never in the same
   envelope as the card.

8. **Record the case reference** and give it to the customer. Any follow-up call about the
   replacement is handled against this reference.

9. **Activate on arrival.** The replacement card is inactive on delivery. The customer
   activates it in the mobile app, at any Libra Bank ATM, or by calling Card Support. The
   old card stops working the moment the new one is activated, not before.

If the customer needs to spend before the replacement arrives, issue a **virtual card**
immediately: it is available in the app within minutes and works for online and wallet
payments.
