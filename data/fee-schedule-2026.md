# Card fee schedule 2026

**This schedule applies to transactions dated 1 January 2026 or later.** For transactions
in 2025 use *Card fee schedule 2025*, which is retained for dispute handling. Several
lines changed at the turn of the year; the changed lines are marked.

All amounts are in lei (RON) unless stated otherwise.

## Monthly maintenance

| Card type | Monthly fee | Waived if |
|---|---|---|
| Libra Standard Debit | 3.00 | monthly card spend ≥ 500 |
| Libra Plus Debit | 5.00 *(was 7.00)* | monthly card spend ≥ 1,000 *(was 1,500)* |
| Libra Gold Credit | 15.00 | monthly card spend ≥ 3,000 *(new waiver)* |
| Libra Virtual | 0.00 | always free |

## Cash withdrawal

| Where | Fee |
|---|---|
| Libra Bank ATM | 0.00 |
| Other bank ATM, in Romania | 1.50 + 0.20% of the amount *(fixed part was 1.00)* |
| ATM abroad, inside the EEA | 6.00 + 0.50% of the amount |
| ATM abroad, outside the EEA | 12.00 + 0.50% of the amount |
| Cash at a Libra Bank counter | 5.00 |

## Card issuing and replacement

| Reason | Fee |
|---|---|
| First card issued with a new account | 0.00 |
| Renewal at expiry | 0.00 |
| Replacement — damaged | 15.00 |
| Replacement — lost or stolen | 25.00 |
| Replacement — fraud confirmed by the bank | 0.00 |
| Replacement — bank error or name change | 0.00 |
| Express courier delivery, on top of the above | 30.00 |
| Emergency replacement abroad | 120.00 *(was 150.00)* |

## Other card fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| PIN change at an ATM | 1.00 |
| PIN reminder by SMS | 0.00 *(was 2.00)* |
| Statement copy, per month requested | 5.00 |
| Transaction dispute, if the claim is rejected as unfounded | 20.00 |
| Foreign currency conversion markup | 2.50% *(was 2.00%)* |

## Late payment, credit cards only

The late payment fee on a Libra Gold Credit card is **50.00 lei** per missed minimum
payment, charged once per statement cycle. This replaced the 40.00 lei fee that applied
in 2025.
