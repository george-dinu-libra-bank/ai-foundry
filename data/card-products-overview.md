# Card products overview

Libra Bank retail customers can hold four card products. This document compares what they
*do*; eligibility is in *Card eligibility criteria* and price is in the current *Card fee
schedule*.

| Feature | Standard Debit | Plus Debit | Gold Credit | Virtual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Funding | current account | current account | credit line | parent card's account |
| Plastic | yes | yes | yes | no |
| Contactless | yes | yes | yes | via wallet only |
| ATM cash | yes | yes | yes | no |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Travel insurance | no | no | yes | no |
| Interest-free period | not applicable | not applicable | up to 55 days | not applicable |
| Free supplementary cards | 0 | 2 | 1 | not applicable |
| Card validity | 4 years | 4 years | 3 years | 1 year |
| Minimum age | 14 | 14 | 18 | 14, with conditions |

## Standard Debit

The default product opened with every current account. Spends what is in the account and
nothing more; there is no overdraft on a Standard Debit card unless an overdraft facility
was separately agreed on the account.

## Plus Debit

The same mechanics as Standard, with higher daily limits, two free supplementary cards for
family members, and a lower monthly fee since 2026. It is a debit card: the "Plus" refers
to limits and included services, not to credit.

## Gold Credit

A credit card with a revolving credit line. Purchases made in a statement cycle carry no
interest if the full statement balance is paid by the due date — up to 55 days depending
on when in the cycle the purchase fell. Cash withdrawals never enjoy the interest-free
period; interest runs from the day of withdrawal. Includes travel insurance for the
cardholder while travelling on a trip where at least half the transport cost was paid with
the card.

## Virtual

See *Libra Virtual card*. Free, instant, no plastic, no cash.

## What we do not issue

Libra Bank retail does not issue prepaid cards, gift cards, business or corporate cards, or
cards denominated in a currency other than lei. Requests for any of these are referred to
the Business Banking desk, which is outside the scope of retail card servicing.
