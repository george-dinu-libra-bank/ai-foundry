# Libra Virtual card

A virtual card is a card number with no plastic. It exists only in the mobile app and in
digital wallets, and it is the fastest product the bank can put in a customer's hands: it
is issued in **under two minutes**, at any hour.

## What it is for

- Spending immediately while a physical replacement is in the post.
- Online shopping on sites the customer does not trust, because the number can be deleted
  and reissued at will.
- Subscriptions the customer wants to be able to kill unilaterally.
- Travel, where it works through Apple Pay or Google Pay at any contactless terminal.

## How it works

The virtual card is funded from the account of a **parent card** — an existing active debit
or credit card. It has its own card number, expiry date and CVV, but it shares the parent
card's account balance. It does **not** have its own daily limits set independently: it
inherits the POS and online limits of the parent card type, and the two together cannot
exceed that limit in a day.

A virtual card cannot withdraw cash. There is no plastic to put in an ATM, and it cannot be
used for cardless ATM withdrawal either.

## Fees

The Libra Virtual card is free: no issuing fee, no monthly maintenance fee, and no fee for
deleting and reissuing it. Transactions on it are charged exactly as they would be on the
parent card, including the foreign currency conversion markup.

## Reissuing and deleting

A customer can delete a virtual card instantly in the app and issue a new one with a
different number. Deleting it does not affect the parent card. There is no limit on how
many times this can be done, but only **three** virtual cards may be active at once per
customer.

Deleting a virtual card kills any subscription billed to it, with no notice to the
merchant — this is the point of the product, and customers should be told so, because a
deleted card that was paying for something important simply stops paying for it.

## Restrictions

- Not available as a customer's only card; see *Card eligibility criteria*.
- Not accepted where a physical card must be presented, such as car hire deposits and some
  hotel check-ins.
- Not available to customers aged 14 to 17 unless the online payment channel has been
  enabled on their Standard Debit card.
