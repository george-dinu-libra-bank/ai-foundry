# Using a card abroad and foreign currency conversion

## No travel notification is needed

Libra Bank removed travel notifications in 2024. Customers do not need to announce a trip,
and the app has no screen for it. Foreign transactions are assessed by the fraud engine on
their own merits. A customer who insists on notifying us should be told plainly that there
is nothing to record and that their card will work.

## How a foreign transaction is converted

A card transaction in a currency other than lei is converted in two steps:

1. The card scheme converts the transaction currency into euro at its own daily rate, on
   the day it processes the transaction — which may be one or two days after the purchase.
2. Libra Bank converts euro into lei at the National Bank of Romania reference rate for
   that processing day, and adds the **foreign currency conversion markup** from the
   current fee schedule.

The consequence customers most often complain about is that the amount debited differs
from the amount they calculated at the till, because the rate used is the rate on the
processing day, not on the purchase day.

## Dynamic currency conversion

Terminals abroad frequently offer to charge in lei instead of the local currency. This is
**dynamic currency conversion**, it is done by the merchant's provider at a rate we do not
control, and it is almost always worse than ours. Always advise the customer to choose the
**local currency**. When they choose lei, no Libra markup is applied — but the merchant's
own margin, typically 3% to 6%, more than replaces it.

## Cash abroad

ATM withdrawals abroad carry both the withdrawal fee from the current fee schedule and the
conversion markup, because the amount must still be converted. Inside the EEA the fee is
lower than outside it. The ATM operator may add its own surcharge, which it must display
before the withdrawal is confirmed and which the bank does not receive and cannot refund.

## Blocked while abroad

A card blocked by the fraud engine abroad is unblocked by calling Card Support on
**+40 21 208 8000** — the free 0800 number does not work from a foreign network. A customer
who cannot call should issue a **virtual card** in the app and pay through Apple Pay or
Google Pay while the block is sorted out.

## Card acceptance

Libra Bank cards work anywhere the scheme is accepted. There are no country blocks on
retail cards. Cards are not usable in jurisdictions under international sanctions, where
the transaction is refused by the scheme rather than by us.
