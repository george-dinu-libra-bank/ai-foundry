# Card daily limits 2026

**These limits apply from 1 January 2026.** They replaced the 2025 limits, and the ATM
cash limits in particular were raised. A dispute about a 2025 transaction is still judged
against *Card daily limits 2025*.

A daily limit is counted over a rolling calendar day in Romanian time, from 00:00 to
23:59, and resets at midnight regardless of when the card was used.

## Standard limits by card type

| Card type | ATM cash per day | POS and online per day | Contactless without PIN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Standard Debit | 8,000 | 15,000 | 150 per transaction |
| Libra Plus Debit | 15,000 | 30,000 | 150 per transaction |
| Libra Gold Credit | 8,000 | 40,000 | 150 per transaction |
| Libra Virtual | not available | 15,000 | not applicable |

## Cumulative contactless limit

After a cumulative **500 lei** of contactless payments without a PIN, the terminal asks
for the PIN on the next contactless transaction whatever its value. Entering the PIN
resets the counter to zero. The 2025 threshold was 300 lei.

## Temporary increases

A customer can raise the ATM cash limit for a single day up to **20,000 lei** from the
mobile app under **Cards → Limits → Temporary increase**, or by calling Card Support. The
increase applies for 24 hours from approval and can be requested at most **three times per
calendar month**. There is no fee.

## Permanent limit changes

A permanent change to a card's limits is a branch request and needs an identity check. A
permanent increase above the standard limit for the card type is assessed against the
customer's account history and can be refused. A permanent *decrease* is always granted,
takes effect the same day, and is the recommended answer for a customer worried about
fraud exposure.

## Where limits do not apply

Transfers made from the mobile app or internet banking are account transfers, not card
transactions, and are not counted against these limits. Direct debits and standing orders
are likewise account-level and unaffected.
