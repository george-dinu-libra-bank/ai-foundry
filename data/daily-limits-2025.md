# Card daily limits 2025

**These limits were in force until 31 December 2025.** They are kept on record because
disputes and chargebacks about 2025 transactions are judged against the limits that
applied on the transaction date, not against today's limits. From 1 January 2026 the
limits in *Card daily limits 2026* apply.

A daily limit is counted over a rolling calendar day in Romanian time, from 00:00 to
23:59, and resets at midnight regardless of when the card was used.

## Standard limits by card type

| Card type | ATM cash per day | POS and online per day | Contactless without PIN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Standard Debit | 5,000 | 10,000 | 100 per transaction |
| Libra Plus Debit | 10,000 | 25,000 | 100 per transaction |
| Libra Gold Credit | 5,000 | 30,000 | 100 per transaction |
| Libra Virtual | not available | 10,000 | not applicable |

## Cumulative contactless limit

After a cumulative **300 lei** of contactless payments without a PIN, the terminal asks
for the PIN on the next contactless transaction whatever its value. Entering the PIN
resets the counter to zero.

## Temporary increases

A customer could raise the ATM cash limit for a single day up to **15,000 lei** by calling
Card Support. The increase applied for 24 hours from approval and could be requested at
most **twice per calendar month**. There was no fee.

## Where limits do not apply

Transfers made from the mobile app or internet banking are account transfers, not card
transactions, and were never counted against these limits. Direct debits and standing
orders are likewise account-level and unaffected.
