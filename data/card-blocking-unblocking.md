# Card blocking and unblocking

A Libra Bank debit or credit card can be blocked in three different ways, and the
route back depends on which one applied.

## Automatic block after failed PIN attempts

A card is blocked automatically after **three consecutive failed PIN attempts** at an
ATM or a POS terminal. The counter resets to zero after any successful PIN entry, so
two wrong attempts followed by a correct one leave the card fully usable. The block is
applied to the card, not to the account: other cards on the same account keep working,
and online payments that do not require a PIN are also stopped, because the block is
applied at the card level in the authorisation system.

An automatic PIN block is removed by the customer without visiting a branch. In Libra
Assist Mobile, open **Cards → your card → Unblock PIN**. The unblock takes effect within
**15 minutes**. There is no fee for removing an automatic PIN block. A customer without
the mobile app must call the Card Support line on 0800 800 210.

## Temporary block requested by the customer

A customer who has mislaid a card but expects to find it can freeze it from the mobile
app under **Cards → Freeze card**. A frozen card declines every transaction but stays
alive: the card number, the expiry date and any standing subscriptions are preserved,
and unfreezing is instant. A temporary block never expires on its own; it stays in place
until the customer lifts it. Freezing and unfreezing are free and unlimited.

## Block applied by the bank

The bank blocks a card on its own initiative when the fraud monitoring system detects a
pattern it cannot accept, when the card is reported lost or stolen, or when a court or
regulatory order requires it. A block of this kind cannot be lifted by the customer. The
customer must call Card Support or visit a branch with an identity document; after
identity verification the agent either lifts the block or issues a replacement card
under the procedure in *Card replacement*.

A bank-applied fraud block is normally accompanied by an SMS to the registered phone
number. If a customer reports a block they did not cause and received no SMS, treat the
registered phone number as possibly out of date and verify it before anything else.

## What a block does not do

A block does not cancel the card contract, does not stop the monthly maintenance fee,
and does not reverse transactions that were already authorised before the block. Direct
debits and standing orders on the current account are unaffected, because they are
account-level instructions and do not travel over the card.
