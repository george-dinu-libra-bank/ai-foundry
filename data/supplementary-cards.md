# Supplementary cards

A supplementary card is a second card on someone else's account, issued to a person the
main cardholder names. The main cardholder remains liable for everything spent on it.

## Who can hold one

The supplementary cardholder must be at least **14 years old** and must be identified by the
bank exactly as a main cardholder would be: a valid identity document, recorded on file. A
supplementary card cannot be issued to someone the bank has never met.

There is no requirement that the two people be related, and no income condition on the
supplementary holder — the account's eligibility was already established by the main
cardholder.

## How many, and what they cost

The number of free supplementary cards depends on the product; see *Card products overview*.
Beyond the free allowance, each supplementary card carries the monthly maintenance fee of
its own card type from the current fee schedule.

## Limits

A supplementary card shares the main card's daily limits and does not add to them. If the
main card's ATM limit is 8,000 lei per day, then the main card and all supplementary cards
together can withdraw 8,000 lei that day, not 8,000 lei each.

The main cardholder can set a **lower** limit on a supplementary card in the app, under
**Cards → the supplementary card → Limits**. A supplementary limit can never exceed the main
card's. This is how parents cap a teenager's spending.

## What the supplementary holder can and cannot do

They can: pay, withdraw cash within the limit, freeze and unfreeze their own card, change
their own PIN, and see their own transactions in their own app.

They cannot: see the main cardholder's transactions, raise limits, order a replacement for
the main card, close the account, or dispute a transaction made on the main card. A dispute
on a supplementary card is raised by the main cardholder.

## Ending it

The main cardholder can close a supplementary card at any time, in the app or in branch,
without the supplementary holder's agreement or knowledge. The supplementary holder can
close their own card but cannot reopen it.

Closing the main card closes every supplementary card on it automatically. The bank does not
notify supplementary holders of this, which is a frequent source of embarrassment at tills
and worth mentioning at closure.
